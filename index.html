Foundation

Nothing pays until Microsoft can see the work.

PAL — Partner Admin Link

  • Links our MPN ID to the customer's Azure credentials for automatic consumption tracking
  • Partner-initiated — set it up ourselves, no customer action required
  • Multiple partners can hold PAL on the same subscription

CPOR — Customer Partner of Record

  • Claims credit for services delivered across Microsoft 365, Dynamics 365, Power Platform and Azure — not just consumption
  • Must be claimed within 120 days of the transactional date
  • Feeds Solutions Partner performance & customer-success scoring

DPOR — Digital Partner of Record (legacy)

  • One partner per subscription, tied to Enterprise Agreements
  • Customer must initiate the association, not the partner
  • Doesn't survive an EA → MCA migration — PAL is the modern replacement
FY27 rule: PAL on every Azure engagement, CPOR on every services engagement — Day 1, not Month 6.

PAL Tagging Playbook

Associates our MPN ID with the customer's Azure subscription so Microsoft attributes consumption to us automatically — even when we didn't sell the licensing.

Gates usage-based incentive eligibility, feeds Solutions Partner performance points (Data & AI, Infrastructure, Digital & App Innovation), and is required for specializations that depend on deployed workloads.
1. Confirm the MPN ID is active in Partner Center. 2. At kickoff, request Owner/Contributor access on the customer's subscription. 3. Link PAL via the Azure portal before first deployment activity. 4. Verify the link shows in Partner Center within 7 days.
Delivery Lead — standing item on every project kickoff checklist.

CPOR Playbook

The formal Microsoft record that Hummingbird is the partner driving a customer's success on a given workload — across Microsoft 365, Dynamics 365, Power Platform and Azure.

Feeds Solutions Partner performance & customer-success scoring, unlocks incentive eligibility tied to workload-level delivery (not just licensing sold), and gives Microsoft visibility to match us into co-sell opportunities.
1. Identify the workload and Microsoft product family at project start. 2. Submit the CPOR claim in Partner Center within 120 days of the transactional date. 3. Track claim status; resolve rejections within the same billing cycle. 4. Re-validate CPOR at renewal or expansion.
Partner Operations — claim within 5 business days of contract signature.

The Frontier Partner Specialization

Replaces the Frontier Partner Badge (retiring end of June 2027). Validates the ability to design, build, deploy, govern and secure AI agents across the full Microsoft stack — M365 Copilot, Azure AI Foundry, GitHub Copilot, Defender, Entra and Purview.

  • 4 prerequisite specializations: M365 Copilot, AI Apps or AI Platform on Azure, Data Security, Identity & Access Management
  • Skilling: 5 people hold the Frontier Transformation Engineer badge; 3 hold Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate (DP-600)
  • Third-party audit across design → build → deploy → govern → secure → operate; 2-year audit cadence
  • Opens for enrollment later in FY27 — start preparing now
  • Eligibility for Frontier-aligned co-sell opportunities
  • Microsoft Agent prepurchase plan credits + Microsoft 365 E7 licensing
  • Packaged go-to-market resources: campaigns & case study opportunities
  • MAICPP Concierge support through the qualification process
Badge holders keep using the badge through FY27 — after June 2027, only the specialization counts. Hummingbird's target: earn it before the badge sunsets.

Co-sell, Marketplace & MACC

Co-sell: getting Microsoft sellers to sell with us

Baseline tier

  • Visible to Microsoft sellers once a solution or practice is published with core information
  • The entry point — no revenue threshold to clear

Higher tier

  • Requires $100K Azure Consumed Revenue (trailing 12 months), technical validation, and a transactable Marketplace offer
  • Unlocks MACC eligibility, Microsoft seller quota credit, and premium Marketplace Rewards
Co-sell and Marketplace are one flywheel in FY27 — Frontier Accelerate for Marketplace formally unifies them starting September 2026.

MACC: Microsoft Azure Consumption Commitment

A contractual commitment enterprise customers make to spend a set amount on Azure over time (typically 1–3 years). Eligible Marketplace purchases automatically decrement it — "MACC burn-down."

How it works

  • Only offers badged "Azure benefit eligible" count
  • Applies to Azure-exclusive licenses only — hybrid or on-prem deployments don't qualify
  • Contribution posts when Microsoft issues the invoice

Why Hummingbird cares

Enterprise buyers actively hunt for MACC-eligible solutions in Q3/Q4 to spend down committed budget. An eligible Hummingbird offer is a faster, friction-free path into that spend — procurement is already approved.
